1. Claim Evaluation: "Traffic is the Problem" Refuted
  • Refuted by Data: 50.1% of orders are late in low traffic, and 53.6% fail in clear weather. Low/Med traffic accounts for 63.4% of total volume.
  • Distance is Non-Significant: Orders under 5 km fail at 55.6% (Chi-square p-value = 0.419). Traffic adds ~3 minutes to severe trips but does not cause baseline failures.
2. Timeline Decomposition: Where Time is Lost 77% Pre-Pickup
  • Pre-Pickup Blowout: 77.4% of excess delay (+14.2 min) occurs before pickup (`created_at` to `pickup_at`). Road transit expands by only +4.1 min (22.6%).
  • Customer Tickets: 49.3% cite kitchen/courier delays; 18.4% cite dynamic ETA creep.
3. Contractual SLA Performance 43.4% On-Time
  • Census Population: Strictly N = 1,532 valid delivered orders (3 DB duplicates removed; 37 missing timestamps recovered).
  • Severe SLA Breach: Actual on-time rate is 43.4% against the 90.0% contractual commitment (-46.6% gap).
  • Delay Magnitude: Median late order is +7.9 min late (mean +13.6 min; tail reaches +50.3 min).
